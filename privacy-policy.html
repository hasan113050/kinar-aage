Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains what information Kinar Aage may collect when you use the website and how that information may be used.

Information You Provide

You may choose to provide information directly to Kinar Aage when using certain website features.

This may include:

  • Your email address when subscribing to the newsletter.
  • Your name, email address, subject and message when submitting the contact form.

Affiliate Link Activity

When certain affiliate or merchant links are clicked, Kinar Aage may record limited information about the click, such as the relevant product, the page or section where the click originated and the time of the interaction.

This information may be used to understand which recommendations are useful and to evaluate website performance.

Saved Products

The Saved Products feature may store selected product information locally in your browser so that your saved items can remain available when you return to the website from the same browser.

This locally stored information is associated with the browser or device you are using and may be removed if you clear your browser storage.

How Information May Be Used

Information collected through Kinar Aage may be used to:

  • Respond to questions or messages submitted through the contact form.
  • Manage newsletter subscriptions and send relevant website updates where applicable.
  • Understand product and affiliate-link engagement.
  • Maintain, improve and troubleshoot website features.
  • Protect the website from misuse or technical problems.

Third-Party Websites

Kinar Aage contains links to third-party merchants, marketplaces and other websites. Once you leave Kinar Aage, the privacy practices of the destination website apply.

We encourage visitors to review the privacy policies and terms of any third-party website before providing personal information or completing a transaction.

Service Providers

Kinar Aage may use third-party technology or infrastructure providers to operate website features, store website data, deliver images or support other technical functionality.

Information processed through those services may be subject to the relevant provider's security and privacy practices.

Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to operate website features, maintain records, respond to enquiries, analyse website activity or meet legitimate operational requirements.

Data Security

Reasonable technical and administrative measures may be used to protect information handled through the website. However, no internet-based system or method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Your Choices

You may choose not to submit optional information through the website. You may also clear locally saved website data through your browser settings.

If you have subscribed to communications and wish to stop receiving them, you may contact Kinar Aage to request removal from the relevant subscriber list.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as the website, its features or its operating practices change. The current version published on this page will apply when you use Kinar Aage.

Contact

If you have a privacy-related question or request, please contact Kinar Aage through the website's Contact page.

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page .
